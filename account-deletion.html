Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: April 2026

How to delete your account

Open the Swypee app, go to Settings → Safety & Privacy, scroll to the Danger Zone section, and tap Delete My Account. You'll be asked to confirm once before deletion starts.

What gets deleted

Your profile, pet profiles, photos, matches, messages, and preferences. Your account is deactivated immediately and all associated data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Need help?

If you can't access the app or need your data deleted another way, email privacy@swypee.app from the email address on your account and we'll process the deletion for you.
