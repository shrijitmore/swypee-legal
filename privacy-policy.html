Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number, pet details, and photos. We also collect usage data such as how you interact with the App, device identifiers, and approximate location (if you grant permission).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: operate and improve the App; match you with other pet owners; send notifications about matches and messages; respond to support requests; and send optional marketing communications (only with your consent).

3. Location Data

If you grant location permission, we use your approximate location to show you nearby pets and to display proximity information to other users. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings or enable Ghost Mode in the App to hide your location from other users.

4. Photos & Media

Pet and profile photos you upload are stored securely on our servers and displayed to other users of the App. We do not use your photos for purposes other than operating the App. You can delete your photos at any time by editing your profile or pet.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers who assist in operating the App (e.g., cloud storage, push notification services) under strict confidentiality agreements.

6. Push Notifications

We use your device token to send push notifications about new matches, messages, and app activity. You can manage notification preferences in App Settings → Notifications, or through your device notification settings.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest) to protect your data. Access to your data is restricted to authorised personnel. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Swypee is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data; object to or restrict processing; and data portability. To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@swypee.app.

11. Cookies & Tracking

The App does not use browser cookies. We may use anonymous analytics to understand how the App is used. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or email. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our Privacy Team at privacy@swypee.app.
